## Selection policy

Project backtest default:

- `completeness_mode = complete_only`
- `exclude_coverage_edges = true`
- only closed bars at or before the canonical cutoff are eligible
- missing feed data never moves a named-session boundary
- named-session membership comes only from the separately validated IANA/DST-aware policy

The alternative diagnostic mode `allow_incomplete_with_flag` may retain incomplete fully evaluable sessions, but their incompleteness remains explicit.

## Important Tokyo consequence

The latest complete Tokyo-policy instance in this dataset is `2026-03-27`. Later Tokyo-policy windows are generally incomplete because expected M5 opens are absent inside the fixed 09:00–18:00 Tokyo-local research window. The adapter does not shift the Tokyo window around those missing bars and does not infer a broker-maintenance explanation.

This means `complete_only` research will have a much smaller Tokyo sample than London or New York on this feed. Any cross-session comparison must report the resulting sample-size asymmetry rather than treating the three sessions as equally observed.
