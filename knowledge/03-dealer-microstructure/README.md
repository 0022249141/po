# Dealer / Market Microstructure

## Canonical academic references named by the map

- Kyle (1985)
- Glosten–Milgrom (1985)
- Ho & Stoll (1981)
- Grossman–Miller (1988)
- arXiv:2003.05958 — *Optimal Market Making with Persistent Order Flow*
- arXiv:2407.17393

## Purpose

Use academic market microstructure to give a defensible theoretical foundation for concepts such as inventory risk, informed trading, liquidity provision and market making.

## Hard boundary

These sources do **not** grant direct visibility into a bank/dealer's current inventory, hidden orders, DOM, CVD, Open Interest or liquidation data. Such observations require actual data feeds.
