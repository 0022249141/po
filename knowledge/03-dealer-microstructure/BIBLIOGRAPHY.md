# Dealer / Market Microstructure — Bibliography

This file records bibliographic metadata and allowed project role. It does not reproduce full copyrighted papers.

## Kyle (1985)

Albert S. Kyle, **“Continuous Auctions and Insider Trading”**, *Econometrica*, 53(6), 1315–1335/1336, 1985. Stable public metadata: Econometric Society / JSTOR record `1913210`.

Project role: informed trading, price informativeness, market depth; theoretical foundation only.

## Glosten & Milgrom (1985)

Lawrence R. Glosten and Paul R. Milgrom, **“Bid, ask and transaction prices in a specialist market with heterogeneously informed traders”**, *Journal of Financial Economics* 14(1), 71–100, 1985. DOI: `10.1016/0304-405X(85)90044-3`.

Project role: adverse selection and bid-ask spread mechanism.

## Ho & Stoll (1981)

Thomas Ho and Hans R. Stoll, **“Optimal dealer pricing under transactions and return uncertainty”**, *Journal of Financial Economics* 9(1), 47–73, 1981. DOI: `10.1016/0304-405X(81)90020-9`.

Project role: dealer inventory risk and quote-setting theory.

## Grossman & Miller (1988)

Sanford J. Grossman and Merton H. Miller, **“Liquidity and Market Structure”**, *The Journal of Finance* 43(3), 617–633, 1988. DOI: `10.1111/j.1540-6261.1988.tb04594.x`.

Project role: supply/demand of immediacy and market-maker risk-bearing.

## Jusselin (2020)

Paul Jusselin, **“Optimal market making with persistent order flow”**, arXiv:`2003.05958`, 2020.

Project role: quantitative inspiration for market making with clustered/persistent order flow; not a direct trading signal for XAUUSD.

## Boyce, Herdegen & Sánchez-Betancourt (2024)

Robert Boyce, Martin Herdegen, Leandro Sánchez-Betancourt, **“Market Making with Exogenous Competition”**, arXiv:`2407.17393`, 2024. DOI record: `10.48550/arXiv.2407.17393`.

Project role: quantitative reference for inventory-aware market making under competition.

## Boundary

These sources justify theory about mechanisms. They do **not** justify claims such as “banks are buying here”, “dealer inventory is short”, or “true order flow confirms X” without corresponding observed data.
