## Verified method scope

The official tutorial explicitly organizes the Wyckoff Method around:

- Five-Step Approach to the Market
- Composite Man
- Wyckoff Price Cycle
- Three Wyckoff Laws
- Trading-range analysis
- Accumulation / Distribution events and phases
- Supply and Demand Analysis
- Relative / Comparative Strength
- Nine Buying/Selling Tests
- Point-and-Figure count guidance

Wyckoff Analytics also describes its foundational curriculum as emphasizing **Price Structural Analysis, Supply and Demand, and Relative and Comparative Strength**.

## Evidence boundary

This establishes native Wyckoff scope and vocabulary. It does not justify mapping Wyckoff events to ICT/SMC or RTM terminology by analogy.

Volume-sensitive Wyckoff conclusions require explicit declaration of the actual volume source. Broker tick volume must not be silently treated as centralized exchange volume.
